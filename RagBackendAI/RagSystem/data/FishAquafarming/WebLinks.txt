Fish Aquafarming and Aquaculture Agriculture Knowledge Base

Expert Knowledge Blocks for Daily Guidance:

[Pond Preparation & Soil]
- Soil pH: Ideal range is 6.5 to 7.5. For acidic soils, apply lime at a rate of 200-500 kg/ha depending on acidity.
- Water Depth: Maintain a minimum of 1.2 to 1.5 meters to ensure temperature stability and oxygen retention.

[Water Quality Parameters]
- Dissolved Oxygen (DO): Should be above 5.0 mg/L. Below 3.0 mg/L causes stress; below 1.0 mg/L leads to mortality.
- Ammonia (NH3): Toxic levels start above 0.05 mg/L. Use probiotics or zeolite to reduce high ammonia.
- pH Stability: Monitor pH daily. Drastic shifts (more than 0.5 units) can cause fish stress and death.

[Seasonal Health Management]
- Summer: High temperatures reduce oxygen solubility. Increase aeration and reduce feeding during peak heat hours.
- Monsoon: Sudden rains can cause pH crashes. Check alkalinity and apply lime after heavy rains.
- Winter: Slow metabolism. Reduce feed quantity by 30-50% and use high-protein, easy-to-digest pellets.

[Common Diseases & Solutions]
- White Spot (Ich): Caused by Ichthyophthirius multifiliis. Treat with Salt (2-3 ppt) or Potassium Permanganate.
- Fin Rot: Bacterial infection. Improve water quality and use anti-bacterial treatments like AquaGuard.
- EUS (Epizootic Ulcerative Syndrome): Deep ulcers. Use lime (CIFAULCER) and avoid high stocking densities.

[Agricultural Integration]
- Aquaponics: Use fish waste water to grow leafy greens. This naturally filters the water for the fish.
- Feed Efficiency: Use the FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio) to monitor profitability. Aim for an FCR of 1.2 to 1.5.
